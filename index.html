The channel

BuildTech Lab is created by Edgar Antonio Reyes Chavez, an individual creator based in Lima, Peru. Videos are produced in English for a U.S. audience and cover megaprojects, engineering disasters, robots and AI builders, and the history of construction. Some videos are made with AI assistance and are labeled as such on each platform.

New videos are published first on YouTube. The same stories later appear on TikTok and Instagram at @buildtech_lab.

BuildTech Lab Uploader

BuildTech Lab Uploader is a private internal script used only by the channel owner. It connects to the BuildTech Lab YouTube channel through YouTube Data API v3 so the owner can upload and schedule videos, set thumbnails and metadata, and add videos to playlists.

The tool is not a public app. It does not collect data from viewers or from anyone other than the authorized channel owner, and it does not show ads. Details of what it accesses, how data is stored, and how access can be revoked are in the Privacy Policy. Use of the tool is also subject to the Terms of Use and the YouTube Terms of Service.
