Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BuildTech Lab Uploader (the “App”) accesses, uses, stores, and shares information. The App is a private internal script operated by Edgar Antonio Reyes Chavez (the “Operator”), an individual creator based in Lima, Peru, for the BuildTech Lab YouTube channel.

Contact for privacy questions or complaints: edgareyes@gmail.com.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services, including YouTube Data API v3, to manage the Operator’s own YouTube channel. By using the App, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Google Privacy Policy, which explains how Google collects and uses information when you authorize the App or when the App calls YouTube API Services.

Powered by YouTube.

2. Who this App is for

The App is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered as a public product, does not have other end users, and does not collect information from YouTube viewers, subscribers, or anyone other than the authorized Operator.

3. Information we access, collect, and store

When the Operator signs in with Google and authorizes the App, the App requests the following YouTube Data API v3 scopes:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to the authorized channel
  • youtube — manage the authorized YouTube account
  • youtube.force-ssl — access and manage YouTube account data over a secure connection, including videos, playlists, and related metadata

Through those scopes, the App may access Authorized Data and other API Data from the Operator’s Google and YouTube account, including:

  • Channel identity and basic channel information, such as the authorized channel name and channel ID
  • The ability to upload and schedule videos
  • Video metadata, such as titles, descriptions, tags, category, language, thumbnail images, and related settings (for example, made-for-kids and AI-disclosure fields)
  • Playlist membership and playlist metadata
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens that confirm the Operator has granted access

The App does not access, collect, or store personal information about channel viewers or about any person other than the authorized Operator. This website is a static informational site and does not itself collect personal information.

4. How we use this information

The App uses this information solely to operate the Operator’s own BuildTech Lab channel, specifically to:

  • Upload and schedule videos
  • Set or update thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and other video metadata
  • Add videos to the Operator’s playlists
  • Confirm that the authorized account is the BuildTech Lab channel
  • Read the processing and privacy status of the Operator’s own videos and list the channel’s recent uploads
  • Delete a video on the Operator’s own channel only when the Operator explicitly runs the delete command (used for private test uploads)

The App does not use this information for advertising, analytics, profiling, resale, or any other secondary purpose that is not described here.

5. Storage

OAuth tokens and any Authorized Data the App retains are stored only on a private computer used exclusively by the Operator, in credential files whose access is restricted to the Operator’s account. The App does not store this data in a shared database and does not provide it to any third-party service.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user information or YouTube API Data with third parties. There is no internal team beyond the Operator, and we do not share this data with external parties except when required by law.

When the App calls YouTube API Services, Google and YouTube process data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Advertising, cookies, and device tracking

The App does not display advertisements and does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements.

The App does not store, access, or collect information directly or indirectly on or from users’ devices by placing, accessing, or recognizing cookies or similar technologies, and it does not use analytics or advertising cookies. This website also does not set cookies or run analytics.

8. Retention and deletion

Authorized Data is kept only for as long as needed to operate the App for the Operator’s channel.

The Operator may delete locally stored tokens and any locally stored Authorized Data at any time by removing the App’s local credential files.

In addition, the Operator can revoke the App’s access to their Google Account at any time through Google’s security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

When access is revoked — either through that Google page or by deleting local credentials — we delete all Authorized Data and API Data related to that authorization as soon as possible, and in any event within 7 calendar days, in accordance with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

9. Contact

If you have questions or complaints about these privacy practices, contact Edgar Antonio Reyes Chavez at edgareyes@gmail.com.
